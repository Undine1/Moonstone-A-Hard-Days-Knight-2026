COPY YOUR OWN MOONSTONE ADF FILES INTO THIS FOLDER

The original game disks are not included.

Use three ADF files, each exactly 901,120 bytes, and rename them:

    Disk1.adf
    Disk2.adf
    Disk3.adf

Then double-click moonstone.exe. On first launch, the runtime extracts the
required boot files from Disk1.adf automatically.
