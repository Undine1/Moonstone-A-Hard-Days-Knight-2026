COPY YOUR OWN MOONSTONE ADF FILES INTO THIS FOLDER

The original game disks are not included.

Use three ADF files, each exactly 901,120 bytes, and rename them:

    Disk1.adf
    Disk2.adf
    Disk3.adf

Then open Moonstone Singleplayer.exe or Moonstone Multiplayer.exe in the game
folder. On first launch, the runtime extracts the required boot files from
Disk1.adf automatically. See README.txt for controls and savegame locations.
